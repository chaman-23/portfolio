      From business problem to actionable insight

      How I work, followed through one real example: the customer churn project.

      1. 1

        Define the problem

        Which telecom customers are likely to leave, and why? A retention team needs a list it can act on, not just a model score.

      2. 2

        Collect data

        7,043 customers with 21 attributes: contract, tenure, services, charges and whether they churned.

      3. 3

        Clean & validate

        Converted TotalCharges to numeric, filled the 11 missing values with the median, dropped the ID column and encoded categories.

      4. 4

        Analyse & diagnose

        Churn is 26.5% overall but 42.7% on month-to-month contracts and 47.4% in a customer's first year.

      5. 5

        Recommend

        Focus retention effort on month-to-month customers in their first year, and test incentives to move them onto longer contracts.

      6. 6

        Measure outcomes

        Not measured in this project. To measure it, I'd track the churn rate of targeted customers against a comparable untargeted group over three months.
